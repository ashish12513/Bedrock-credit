# BEDROCK SENTINEL - EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1-PAGE)

---

## 🎯 What Is This?

**Bedrock Sentinel** is an AI cost management platform that gives you complete control over Claude AI spending across your organization. It's like a "smart thermostat" for AI - monitoring usage, enforcing budgets, and finding cost savings automatically.

---

## ❌ Problem → ✅ Solution

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **No visibility** into AI costs | Real-time dashboard showing all spending |
| **No spending limits** - bills surprise you | Set monthly/daily quotas per account |
| **Can't optimize** - wasting money | AI recommends cheaper models, saves 30-40% |
| **No audit trail** - compliance risk | Tracks all usage, generates audit reports |

---

## 🚀 What It Does (Features)

✅ **Dashboard** - See all Claude spending in one place  
✅ **Budgets** - Set limits per account/team/model  
✅ **Controls** - Enable/disable Claude for specific environments  
✅ **Chatbot** - Integrated Claude in sidebar with cost tracking  
✅ **Intelligence** - AI suggests cost optimizations  
✅ **Alerts** - Get notified before going over budget  
✅ **Multi-Account** - Manage unlimited AWS accounts centrally
